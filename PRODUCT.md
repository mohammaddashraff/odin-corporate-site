## Product Purpose

ODIN Software Solutions provides software solutions around each client's needs. The website introduces the company and helps prospective clients discuss a project.

## Users

Inferred from the brief: business owners and teams evaluating software for operations, people, customers, education, or a new digital product.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed offerings: HR systems, ERP, CRM, LMS, education systems, mobile applications, and software solutions tailored to a client's needs.
Existing implementation: Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS; English and Arabic language switching. GitHub repository: mohammaddashraff/odin-corporate-site, production branch main, hosted on Vercel at odin-ltd.com.
Open decisions: commercial packages, specific commitments for each project, verified client references, and additional delivery integrations. Do not invent these.

## Brand Commitments

The user explicitly changed the identity to ODIN Software Solutions and selected a professional light identity using navy and blue. They selected direct implementation in code followed by reviewing the working website.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing ODIN brand assets in public/brand and ERP screenshots in public/screenshots. Existing ERP demo URL is retained as one solution example, not the company-wide offer. Contact email from current site: hello@odin-ltd.com.
The repository contains older case-study and testimonial claims; they are not newly verified evidence and should not become proof for the broader company.

## Product Principles

- Begin with the client's needs and workflow.
- Make the breadth of software offerings easy to understand.
- Treat ERP as one solution within the company.
- Use accurate content and concrete examples without invented customer or performance claims.
